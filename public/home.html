My Logic

I decompose before I build. I look for the signal in the noise. Every system has leverage points — I find them, name them, and design around them. Whether I am architecting backend infrastructure, integrating AI into real workflows, or writing an essay that clarifies a messy topic, the same process applies: understand the forces at play, remove accidental complexity, and leave behind something that feels inevitable.
